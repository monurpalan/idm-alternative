## Short description (one sentence)

A free, open-source IDM alternative for Windows and Linux that splits files into parts to
download faster and takes over the browser's downloads, videos and torrents.

## Description

Shardload is a download manager in the spirit of Internet Download Manager, free and open
source, with no ads, trial or account.

- Downloads each file over up to 16 connections at once; when one slows down, a free one takes
  over the rest of its part, so the end does not drag. Resumes after a crash or restart and
  checks files against the publisher's checksum.
- Takes over downloads from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox, with their cookies.
  Alt+click leaves one to the browser.
- Videos from YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok, Twitch and many more (through yt-dlp, set up and
  updated by the app), and HLS/DASH streams with a choice of quality, audio and subtitles.
  Playlists and channels, clips (only 1:05–2:30 of a video), and live recording.
- Torrents and magnet links in the same list; mirrors and Metalink; single files out of a ZIP
  without downloading the archive; a site grabber.
- Categories, queue, scheduler, batch downloads, clipboard monitoring, speed limits, virus scan,
  shut down when done.
- Remembers what each site allows and what you downloaded before; tells where a file came from.
- An MCP server lets AI assistants manage downloads; `shard` on the command line.
- English and Turkish.
